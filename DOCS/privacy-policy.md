# Privacy Policy for RegaloApp

**Last Updated**: January 18, 2026

## Introduction

RegaloApp ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, name, username, birthdate
- **Profile Information**: Avatar (emoji), hobbies, gift preferences
- **User-Generated Content**: Manual birthday entries, group messages, gift information

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data**: App interactions, features used, session duration
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, app version
- **Analytics**: Anonymous usage statistics via Firebase Analytics

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the app's functionality
- Send birthday reminders and notifications
- Facilitate connections between users
- Coordinate group gift-giving
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Analyze usage patterns (anonymized)

## Data Storage and Security

- **Storage**: Your data is stored securely using Firebase/Firestore
- **Encryption**: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Access Control**: Only you can access your personal data
- **Retention**: Data is retained until you delete your account

## Data Sharing

We **DO NOT**:
- Sell your personal information
- Share your data with third parties for marketing
- Use your data for advertising purposes

We **MAY** share data only:
- With your explicit consent
- To comply with legal obligations
- To protect our rights and safety

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: View all your personal data
- **Correct**: Update inaccurate information
- **Delete**: Permanently delete your account and all data
- **Export**: Request a copy of your data (contact us)
- **Opt-out**: Disable notifications at any time

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Firebase/Firestore**: Data storage and authentication
- **Firebase Analytics**: Anonymous usage analytics
- **Expo Push Notifications**: Birthday reminders

These services have their own privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

RegaloApp is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.

## Contact Us

For privacy concerns or data requests:
- **Email**: privacy@regaloapp.com
- **In-App**: Settings > Privacy > Contact Support

## GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are in the EU, you have additional rights under GDPR:
- Right to data portability
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to object to processing
- Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Know if personal information is sold or disclosed
- Opt-out of the sale of personal information
- Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights
